# 📊 Tableau Data Visualization Portfolio

Welcome to my **Tableau Portfolio**.

This folder showcases my Tableau projects, including business dashboards and exploratory data analysis. The projects cover different datasets and business contexts, with visualizations designed to identify patterns, compare performance, and communicate data-driven insights.

Some dashboards include interactive filters, such as date filters, while others are designed as static visual summaries of key findings.

---

## 🚀 Projects

### 1. 🛍️ Sales Performance & Inventory Tracking Dashboard — Toko Gayanara

A dashboard analyzing product sales and inventory performance at Toko Gayanara. It explores stock versus total sales by product, return rates by courier, revenue distribution by city, customer segmentation, top products, and yearly revenue trends.

**Key Skills / Tools:** Tableau, Data Visualization, Sales Analysis, Inventory Analysis, Customer Segmentation, Geographic Analysis, Dashboard Design

---

### 2. 🌐 Global Tech Layoffs & Funding Analysis (2020–2023)

A global analysis of technology layoffs and funding from 2020 to 2023. The dashboard explores the relationship between funds raised and layoffs, the industries and companies most affected, the geographic distribution of layoffs, and trends over time.

**Key Skills / Tools:** Tableau, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Correlation Analysis, Industry Analysis, Geographic Visualization, Trend Analysis, Dashboard Design

---

### 3. 🏠 Airbnb Dashboard

A dashboard exploring Airbnb listing data, including average price by bedroom count, revenue trends, price differences by ZIP code, and the distribution of listings across locations.

**Key Skills / Tools:** Tableau, Data Visualization, Pricing Analysis, Geographic Analysis, Trend Analysis, Dashboard Design

---

### 4. 📈 Sales Dashboard

A sales dashboard presenting revenue by market, quantity by market, revenue over time, top products, top customers, and sales performance metrics. The dashboard includes date controls to support exploration across time periods.

**Key Skills / Tools:** Tableau, Sales Analysis, Time-Series Analysis, Market Analysis, Product Analysis, Customer Analysis, Interactive Date Filters

---

### 5. 🏥 Klinik Medinara Dashboard

A dashboard exploring clinic operations using appointment, diagnosis, doctor, patient, and prescription data. Visualizations include doctor workload, patient complaints, appointment patterns, frequently prescribed medicines, patient return behavior, blood-group distribution, insurance usage, and no-show patterns.

**Key Skills / Tools:** Tableau, Data Visualization, Healthcare Analytics, Operational Analysis, Patient Analysis, Dashboard Design
